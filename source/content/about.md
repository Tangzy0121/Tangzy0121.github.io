# About

Tang Zhengyu / 唐政宇
Undergraduate Researcher, Harbin Institute of Technology

I am an undergraduate researcher at Harbin Institute of Technology. My work spans database systems, machine learning for combinatorial optimization, AI safety, and scientific machine learning. Across these areas I care about one question: how to make intelligent systems reliable and scalable enough to trust in practice.

Currently I work on risk-aware cardinality estimation (SafeCE), decomposition-based combinatorial optimization for database problems (Anyscale-DBCO), and cascading safety failures in humanoid robotic systems (Asimov Cascade).
